# Who Is the Agent?

A hands-on exploration of human, machine, and AI agent identity.

## What I'm exploring

Identity systems answer a few deceptively simple questions:

- Who are you?
- How do we know?
- What are you allowed to do?
- On whose behalf are you acting?

AI agents make those questions more complicated.

This repository is where I'm learning by building small experiments around human identity, machine identity, authentication, authorization, delegation, and agent identity.

## The project

I'll gradually build a small AI agent that can use tools and access resources.

Then I'll make the identity problem harder.

Who authenticates the agent? What can it access? How does it act on behalf of a user? What happens when it calls another service or agent? How do we restrict, revoke, and audit its access?

The goal isn't to build a production system. It's to understand the identity problems by encountering them.
